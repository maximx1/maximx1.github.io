Privacy policy for JWTV and JWTV Music

Last updated 10 November 2026.

JWTV (movies and TV) and JWTV Music are made by Justin Walrath for his family. They are not sold, have no ads, and are not offered to the public. Both apps are clients for a private media server that Justin runs himself.

What the apps collect

The apps talk only to that server. To use them you sign in with an account on it. The apps send it:

  • your account name, to sign you in;
  • what you watch or listen to, how far you got, and how often you've played something, so you can carry on where you left off and see what you've played recently, on every device you sign in on;
  • your settings that live on your account, such as preferred audio language and playlists.

All of this is stored on Justin's server, not by any other company. Photos you add to JWTV Music's themes stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Casting to a TV

If you choose to play something on a Chromecast or Google TV, the app gives that device the address of the video or song on the server, and the device fetches it from there. This uses Google's Cast service on your home network.

Who it's shared with

No one. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or analytics, or given to other companies.

Deleting your data

Ask Justin at walrathjaw@gmail.com and your account and everything stored with it will be deleted. Uninstalling the app removes what it keeps on your device.
