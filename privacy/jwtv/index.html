Privacy policy for JWTV and JWTV Music

Last updated 20 November 2026.

JWTV (movies and TV) and JWTV Music are made by Justin Walrath for his family. They are not sold, have no ads, and are not offered to the public. Both apps are clients for a private media server that each person runs themselves.

How the apps work

You point the app at your own server, sign in with an account on it, and all of your data — account name, watch history, settings, and playlists — lives exclusively on your server. JWTV does not host, collect, or store any of your information. Photos added to JWTV Music's themes stay on your device.

Casting to a TV

If you play something on a Chromecast or Google TV, the app gives that device the address of the media on your server, and the device fetches it from there. This uses Google's Cast service on your home network.

TMDB API (opt in)

Your server deployment can optionally use an API token to fetch movie and show metadata (titles, posters) from TMDB. Your own content is never sent to TMDB, and only the metadata queries you explicitly enable are made. This is an opt in on your server.

Who it's shared with

No one. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or analytics, or given to other companies.

Deleting your data

If you want to delete anything, remove the account from your server. JWTV does not hold any of your data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes what the app keeps on your device.
